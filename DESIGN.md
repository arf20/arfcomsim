# Design

## Lore

The game opens with an undeveloped agrarian and industrial econmy in shambles after
a devastating war. The communist party just took power and has the responsibility of
preventing (or stopping) a famine, develop the economy as quicky as possible in the
service of the material conditions of the workers.

The communist party controls a country which is free of external influence, thus
there is little need for defence (for now).

## Economy

The economy begins with few inputs and outputs. The player has to grow tech trees
not just qualitatively but quantitatively too, i.e. developing power of mass production of
goods.

"To each according to his ability, to each according to his need":
The objective is to meet the demands of the workers, ensure their continued and decent existance.
The needs are organized in tiers. Developing the economy allows meeting more complex needs,
realizing the socialist utopia.

The economy is divided in sectors, each one with a tech tree. They meet different but
correlated demands. Outputs from one can be used in inputs of another.

The player controls a general design of the economy organized in five year plans,
and the adjustment of the implementation with manual and automatic tools for it.

When computer systems are developed, economic planning and adjusting can be increasily
automated with computer algorithms.

## Design

The initial characteristics of the economy are described in a map file. The game save
format follows the same format. This map also contains lore and assets.

The engine must be as generic as reasonable to load different kinds of maps.
